The source URL called “bitwars” in the brief currently identifies **PixelWars**. The original distinction here is a community proposal becoming a validated new behavior definition, with visible checks and actual deployment. Offline fixtures demonstrate the pipeline, not original AI generation. Live-provider verification is still needed to demonstrate the full differentiator publicly.
